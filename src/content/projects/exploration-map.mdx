## Overview

Atlas brings the exploration mechanic of a game into the real world. The map starts under a fog of war; places you visit and journeys you add gradually reveal the world you have discovered.

## The Challenge

A travel history can take many forms: individual places, a road journey, a rail trip, a recorded GPS track, or an area explored on foot. Atlas brings these experiences together in one map and makes progress visible.

## Approach & Development

The experience supports several ways to record exploration:

- Visit places and reveal their surroundings.
- Add road or rail journeys.
- Upload GPS tracks from recorded activities.
- Draw explored routes or areas manually.

These inputs contribute to a shared view of the explored world. Progress is expressed through the area of land explored and the countries visited.

## Final Result

A live, interactive world map that turns travel into an ongoing record of discovery. Visitors can open Atlas to explore the interface and start adding their own journeys.
